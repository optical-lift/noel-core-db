# V46 — Regime-Conditioned Grammar Recombination Test

**Date frozen:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS / PREREGISTRATION — frozen before V46 scoring  
**Repository:** `optical-lift/noel-core-db`  
**Database lineage:** existing Noel anonymous Hebrew structural-state corpus and V41–V44 holdout lineage  
**Masoretic / semantic information:** prohibited  
**V45 reservation:** preserved. V44 reserved V45 for a different trigger condition; this experiment is therefore numbered V46 rather than retroactively reusing V45.

## 1. Working hypothesis

The 65 observable anonymous structural states are generated by a reusable, partially factorized dynamical grammar with at least two separable layers:

```text
slow structural regime Z
        ↓
regime-conditioned local grammar
        ↓
short ordered motif / context
        ↓
observable 65-state sequence
```

The central V46 prediction is not merely that latent regimes or motifs predict the sequence. It is that **a local motif learned in some regimes can be recombined with a regime learned through other motifs, and the resulting unseen regime × motif combination remains predictively recoverable**.

The decisive claim is therefore:

> A factorized regime-conditioned grammar will predict held-out continuations for regime × motif combinations that were never observed together during training, because motif behavior and regime modulation are reusable components rather than memorized pair identities.

V46 is allowed to fail in several informative ways: no stable regimes, no transferable motifs, no regime modulation, pair-specific memorization only, or no advantage beyond ordinary local statistics.

## 2. Structural universe

V46 inherits, without modification, the existing anonymous structural alphabet and corpus lineage:

- 65 anonymous structural states;
- 64 preserved high-frequency intrinsic structural classes plus `OTHER` as state 65;
- ordered token sequence from the existing Noel structural instrument;
- existing book / block identities used only for splitting, support control, and replication;
- no semantic labels, conventional mark names, grammatical labels, translations, theology, Song-function assignments, or manual interpretation of state identities.

No state may be merged, renamed, regrouped, or dropped after V46 results are inspected.

## 3. Holdout discipline

The original V41–V44 outer holdout remains sealed during V46 model construction and regime / motif discovery.

Previously recovered corpus counts are retained as lineage checks:

- ordered structural tokens: 306,785;
- valid nine-token centers: 305,177;
- non-outer-holdout centers: 233,929;
- original outer-holdout centers: 71,248.

V46 uses only the non-outer-holdout material until all of the following are frozen:

1. regime representation;
2. motif representation;
3. support thresholds;
4. candidate model family;
5. hyperparameters / selection rules;
6. regime × motif holdout construction;
7. endpoints;
8. pass / interpretation rules.

Within the non-outer-holdout material, intact blocks are deterministically assigned to:

- **development:** 70%;
- **internal replication:** 30%.

Assignment is by a deterministic hash of the preserved block identifier with seed `46`; no token-level random split is permitted.

The internal replication partition may not be used for hyperparameter selection.

The original 71,248-center outer holdout is opened only after internal replication results are frozen and written.

## 4. Fundamental prediction target

At each eligible position `t`, the model predicts the next four observable structural states:

```text
R_t = x[t+1 : t+4]
```

Primary scoring uses soft-target cross-entropy over the four-token outgoing state repertoire, matching the V44 four-right-state target concept where applicable.

Secondary endpoints include:

- next-state CE;
- next-2 joint / sequential CE;
- next-4 sequential CE;
- outgoing repertoire CE;
- outgoing entropy error;
- predicted probability of a high structural-boundary event within the next four positions.

No single secondary endpoint may rescue failure of the primary recombination endpoint.

## 5. Motif definition

A **motif** is an ordered recent structural context ending at the current position.

Candidate motif lengths are frozen as:

```text
m ∈ {2, 3, 4, 6}
```

The actual motif representation is a variable-order context with deterministic backoff:

- use the longest supported suffix among lengths 6, 4, 3, 2;
- support is measured on development training data only;
- minimum occurrence support for an exact motif is **100** training instances;
- if the longest suffix has support <100, back off to the next shorter candidate;
- if length-2 support is <100, the instance is not eligible for exact-motif recombination analysis but remains eligible for general predictive baselines.

No motif is selected because it appears interesting after scoring.

## 6. Regime definition

V46 assumes the possibility of persistent latent regimes and therefore fits a **duration-aware hidden switching model** rather than defining regimes from semantic or manually chosen structural properties.

Primary regime family:

- hidden semi-Markov / duration-aware switching model;
- observations: only the ordered 65-state sequence;
- candidate regime counts:

```text
K ∈ {4, 6, 8, 12, 16}
```

- regime count selected using development validation likelihood only;
- deterministic training seeds: `46`, `460`, `4600`;
- final regime posterior is the mean / consensus across those frozen seeds under the chosen K;
- regime labels are aligned between seeds using emission-profile matching;
- maximum modeled regime duration: 64 tokens;
- no book identity, block identity, semantic label, center role, or V43/V44 result is provided as a regime feature.

If exact HSMM execution is unavailable in the authorized compute environment, V46 remains unadjudicated rather than substituting a different regime model after results are visible. A pre-scoring implementation-equivalent duration-aware formulation may be documented before training if mathematically identical in target distribution.

## 7. Regime assignment for recombination analysis

Each eligible motif occurrence receives a regime posterior from the frozen regime model using only permitted sequence information.

For the primary recombination analysis, an occurrence receives a discrete regime label only if:

- maximum posterior regime probability ≥0.70.

Occurrences below 0.70 remain usable in probabilistic model fitting but are excluded from discrete regime × motif cell eligibility.

The 0.70 threshold is frozen before results.

## 8. Eligible regime × motif cells

A regime × motif cell `(Z, M)` is eligible for deliberate combination withholding only if all are true in the development material:

- motif M has ≥300 total supported occurrences;
- regime Z has ≥1,000 high-confidence occurrences;
- cell `(Z,M)` has ≥50 occurrences before withholding;
- motif M appears in at least **3** distinct regimes with ≥50 occurrences each;
- regime Z contains at least **20** distinct eligible motifs;
- the cell occurs in at least **3** intact blocks.

If fewer than 20 eligible cells exist, the decisive unseen-combination test is reported **NOT EVALUABLE** rather than weakening thresholds.

## 9. Deterministic combination holdout

For every eligible `(Z,M)` cell selected into the recombination test:

- remove **all development-training occurrences** of motif M assigned to regime Z from the factorized model's interaction-learning objective;
- preserve occurrences of M in all other regimes;
- preserve occurrences of Z containing all other motifs;
- preserve the raw sequence for regime-model inference itself, because the regime definition must remain common and independent of which grammar pair is being tested;
- never expose the withheld `(Z,M)` continuation targets to the grammar interaction learner.

The held-out cell is therefore:

```text
seen motif M
+
seen regime Z
+
unseen M × Z combination for continuation learning
```

Cell selection is deterministic using seed `460046` after eligibility is computed and before outcomes are scored.

If the number of eligible cells is large, cap the decisive set at 200 cells using that deterministic ordering. All remaining eligible cells may be reported secondarily but cannot redefine the primary set.

## 10. Model families

All models predict the same outgoing target.

### M0 — Frequency baseline

```text
P(R)
```

Global structural-state frequency / repertoire baseline learned from development training.

### M1 — Local motif baseline

```text
P(R | M)
```

Variable-order motif model with the frozen deterministic backoff.

### M2 — Regime-only baseline

```text
P(R | Z)
```

Uses frozen regime posterior / identity but no motif.

### M3 — Memorized pair upper-reference

```text
P(R | Z, M)
```

Exact pair-specific table with smoothing where the pair was observed. It is **not eligible** to predict deliberately unseen `(Z,M)` cells except through its frozen fallback. It serves as a reference for seen-combination performance and memorization capacity.

### M4 — Factorized regime-conditioned grammar — PRIMARY

The primary model is forced to share information across motifs and regimes:

```text
logit P(s | M,Z)
 = b_s
 + u_s(M)
 + v_s(Z)
 + <A_s e_M, B_s e_Z>
```

where:

- `e_M` is a learned motif embedding;
- `e_Z` is a learned regime embedding / posterior projection;
- interaction rank is fixed at **8**;
- there is no independent parameter table for exact `(M,Z)` identities;
- motif and regime main effects remain available when the pair is unseen;
- low-dimensional interaction is shared across every eligible combination.

Frozen optimization:

- motif embedding dimension: 16;
- regime embedding dimension: 8;
- interaction rank: 8;
- AdamW;
- learning rate: 0.001;
- weight decay: 0.0001;
- batch size: 2,048;
- max epochs: 200;
- early stopping patience: 10;
- seeds: 46, 460, 4600;
- prediction averaged across seeds;
- no architecture sweep after internal replication is opened.

## 11. Primary endpoint — unseen regime × motif recombination

For every deliberately withheld `(Z,M)` cell, calculate four-right-state CE for:

- M0 frequency;
- M1 motif only;
- M2 regime only;
- M4 factorized regime-conditioned grammar.

Define the strongest noncompositional comparator per cell as:

```text
B = min(CE(M1), CE(M2))
```

Primary improvement:

```text
ΔCE_recombine = CE(B) - CE(M4)
```

Positive values favor reusable regime × motif recombination.

### Decisive recombination gate

The central V46 hypothesis passes internal replication only if all are true:

1. aggregate `ΔCE_recombine > 0`;
2. relative CE reduction of M4 vs the aggregate best noncompositional comparator is ≥ **1%**;
3. 99% block-bootstrap CI for aggregate `ΔCE_recombine` excludes zero;
4. at least **70%** of eligible withheld cells individually favor M4 over their best M1/M2 comparator;
5. the real factorized assignment beats the 99th percentile of the frozen recombination null described below.

The same criteria are then evaluated unchanged on the original outer holdout for final confirmation where corresponding cells are testable.

## 12. Recombination null

The null must preserve the existence of motifs and regimes while breaking their learned reusable correspondence.

Use **1,000 deterministic nulls**, seed `46004600`.

Within regime-frequency strata, permute the mapping between learned regime embeddings and regime labels while preserving:

- motif identities and counts;
- regime frequencies;
- block membership;
- continuation targets;
- motif support;
- withheld-cell structure.

The null asks whether the learned regime modulation is specifically matched to the real regimes rather than merely adding generic low-dimensional parameters.

Primary null statistic: aggregate unseen-cell `ΔCE_recombine`.

## 13. Same motif, different regime transfer

For motifs represented in at least three eligible regimes, measure continuation-distribution divergence across regimes.

Then test whether the change for an unseen `(Z,M)` cell is predictable from:

- how M behaves in other regimes;
- how Z modulates other motifs.

Report:

- CE gain of M4 over M1;
- correlation between predicted and observed regime-specific continuation shifts;
- cell-level sign accuracy for the top state-probability shifts.

This is supporting evidence for transferable regime modulation.

## 14. Same regime, new motif transfer

Symmetrically test whether a regime learned through many motifs can modulate a motif withheld specifically from that regime.

This is operationally the same held-out combination viewed from the regime side, but results are stratified by:

- motif frequency;
- regime frequency;
- motif length;
- regime posterior confidence;
- repertoire diversity.

A result that exists only for the highest-frequency motifs must be reported as such.

## 15. Counterfactual regime substitution

For each withheld-cell evaluation instance, hold motif M and observed continuation R fixed and score all K candidate regimes under M4.

Rank the actual inferred regime.

Report:

- median regime rank;
- MRR;
- top-1;
- top-3;
- full rank distribution.

Counterfactual regime specificity is supporting, not sufficient by itself to pass V46.

## 16. Counterfactual motif substitution

Within the actual regime, score all eligible motifs of the same frozen motif-length class against the observed continuation.

Report actual-motif rank, MRR, and top-decile rate.

This distinguishes regime information from motif information and tests whether the local component is specifically identified.

## 17. Regime-transition grammar — exploratory but frozen

V46 additionally records whether motif behavior depends on an inferred regime transition:

```text
Z_t -> Z_(t+1)
```

For high-confidence transitions with ≥100 development occurrences, compare:

```text
P(R | M, Z_t)
vs.
P(R | M, Z_t, Z_(t+1))
```

and directional transition pairs:

```text
A -> B
B -> A
```

These endpoints are exploratory within V46 and cannot rescue the central unseen-combination gate.

They are frozen now so that a strong transition effect is not invented after inspecting failures elsewhere.

## 18. Unseen regime-transition recombination — exploratory

Where support permits, withhold an ordered regime transition from the transition-conditioned grammar while retaining both individual regimes elsewhere.

Minimum eligibility:

- each regime ≥1,000 high-confidence occurrences;
- ordered transition ≥50 occurrences across ≥3 blocks;
- both regimes participate in at least two other eligible transitions.

Report whether low-dimensional regime geometry predicts the withheld ordered transition's continuation behavior.

If fewer than 20 eligible transitions exist, report NOT EVALUABLE.

## 19. Center redundancy audit

V46 directly revisits the V44 diagnostic under the fuller architecture.

Compare:

```text
P(R | M,Z)
```

with:

```text
P(R | M,Z,y)
```

where `y` is the current individual center state.

The center-added model must use a shared regularized center feature and may not introduce `(M,Z,y)` lookup parameters.

Prediction under the working hypothesis:

- isolated center identity contributes little after motif + regime are known;
- the motif + regime interaction carries substantially more information than center identity alone.

This is a mechanistic audit, not a prerequisite for the main recombination claim.

## 22. Anti-leakage prohibitions

After V46 scoring begins, the following are forbidden:

- changing the 65-state alphabet;
- redefining a motif because another length performs better on replication/test;
- adding motif lengths outside `{2,3,4,6}`;
- changing minimum motif support of 100;
- changing recombination cell thresholds;
- changing posterior confidence threshold 0.70;
- changing candidate K set;
- changing interaction rank 8;
- dropping difficult regimes, motifs, blocks, books, or states;
- selecting only concentrated or low-diversity windows after seeing performance;
- moving examples between development, internal replication, and outer holdout;
- using semantic interpretation to engineer a feature;
- replacing the factorized architecture after seeing held-out results;
- changing the primary endpoint or 1% effect threshold.

Any such change creates a new experiment.

## 23. Interpretation ladder

### A. M4 does not beat motif-only or regime-only baselines

Conclusion: no evidence that regime and motif behave as reusable interacting components. Local statistics and/or latent regimes may still exist separately.

### B. M4 improves seen combinations but not deliberately unseen combinations

Conclusion: regime × motif interaction is present but appears pair-specific / memorized rather than compositional.

### C. M4 predicts unseen combinations but real regime assignment fails the null

Conclusion: low-dimensional factorization helps, but the claimed regime identities are not specifically supported.

### D. Internal replication passes but original outer holdout fails

Conclusion: development-lineage recombination does not generalize to the pre-existing corpus holdout; do not claim reusable grammar.

### E. Internal replication and outer holdout both pass the decisive recombination gate

Permitted claim:

> Anonymous structural motifs and larger latent regimes exhibit reusable, factorized interaction: motifs learned in other regimes and regimes learned through other motifs can be recombined to predict continuations for regime × motif combinations that were never jointly observed by the grammar learner.

Stronger permitted interpretation:

> The observable 65-state sequence is better described as a regime-conditioned local grammar than as either independent local motifs or persistent regimes alone.

Not permitted from V46 alone:

- semantic interpretation;
- intentional authorship claims;
- theological claims;
- biological or physical causal mechanism;
- claim that the recovered regimes are the unique true latent states;
- claim that the factorization is a complete algebra.

## 24. Forward trigger

Only if the decisive unseen-combination result confirms on the original outer holdout should the next experiment test whether regime modulation and motif transformations obey a lower-dimensional algebra capable of predicting **novel transformations and novel regime transitions from other learned relations**.

That next experiment would ask whether the reusable components are merely statistical factors or members of a compositional transformation system.

## 25. Frozen expectation

Before scoring, the active expectation is:

1. motif-only models will carry substantial short-range information;
2. regime-only models will carry independent longer-range information;
3. factorized motif + regime interaction will outperform either alone;
4. the advantage will survive deliberate withholding of exact regime × motif combinations;
5. isolated center identity will add little once motif + regime are known;
6. some of the strongest interaction will occur near regime transitions rather than uniformly inside regimes.

The experiment is designed to distinguish those expectations from pair memorization, local recurrence, frequency structure, and arbitrary latent clustering.
