A separate scope from G2 attestation and P1 proposal packets grants that Person the complete selected Case through one bounded reader. Grant and revocation are immutable. Every read checks current owner and reviewer Reality/Auth bindings, exact Case and purpose, current unexpired permission, and revocation in source, including export/search/history alternatives. The owner interface must plainly identify the Case and amount of data before grant. Revocation stops later reads; it cannot retract what a reviewer already saw.

Acceptance: two reviewers with disjoint Case grants, third signed outsider, expiry and revocation races, wrong Case and alternate route denial, pagination and no general roster/aggregate leak. Audit logs and HTTP errors for financial payload leakage. No actual financial records until protected release and 10D.

It distinguishes original evidence from extracted or user asserted values and preserves the knowledge date. Normalize 2025–2026 transactions, obligations, debts and cash claims into proposed Case items, with deduplication across statements, transfers, Stripe/PayPal and manual entries. Keep household, Elm and Optical Lift distinct while linking cross-Body funding evidence. Unknown and missing evidence remain explicit. Each original baseline line must be shown for owner accept/correct/unknown, with correction as a new proposal; source ingestion alone writes no permanent Carry event or adopted revision. Independent approval remains required for every canonical event under 4D.

Acceptance: synthetic overlapping statements reproduce the same source hashes and proposed items, never duplicate a transfer/payment, never promote projected cash into held cash, preserve original versus derived provenance and event knowledge time, and require exact every-line owner confirmation before independently approved adoption.
