6. **Privacy:** your notes, another student's notes, an individual student submission, teacher feedback and course-wide discussions have distinguishable source owners/audiences; no transparent replication of private responses into a group thread.

8. **Identity/data:** no real Person IDs, emails, credentials, research bodies, lesson manuscript text or private student notes may be embedded in public migration/tests.
9. **Storage/release:** exact new source operation (if necessary) is a shared source migration reviewed in **noel-core-db** and tested on a disposable protected clone; Titus/Atlas only consume signed, narrow DTOs after independent source release. **No new Titus table or service-role authorization**.
