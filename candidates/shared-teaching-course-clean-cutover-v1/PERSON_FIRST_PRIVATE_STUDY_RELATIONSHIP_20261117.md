## 2. Required operations, each independent

| Transition | Actor and evidence required | Result; explicitly NOT implied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Person/current-session resolution | Current genuine signed auth → exact active Reality canonical Person; author bound to original Course, intended learner must independently establish unique current Person. | Authentication only; no teacher, student or materials entitlement from name/login. |
| Author proposes private study | Exact author of same original Course, exact named canonical learner, Course scope, verifiable purpose, idempotent request. | A pending invitation; no acceptance/enrollment, no material title/unit leak until separately permitted. |
| Learner receives/reviews invitation | Only named independently signed recipient; minimal legitimate Course description permitted by author; denial hides even existence from other People. | Invitation display only, not a Course or research read. |
| Learner accepts or declines | Learner's **own independently signed** action on invitation; preserve immutable decision, terminal replay and expiry rules. | Scoped accepted/declined participation relationship candidate. Neither choice makes a teacher's Work assignment or auto-releases Classes. |
| Author releases one specific Unit edition | Independently verified current author, exact legitimate participating learner and immutable issued Unit/edition/revision; separate author disclosure decision and its history. | Exact permitted Unit material only. No full manuscript, other Unit title, citations outside scope, author research, learner roster or another Person's notebook. |
| Learner reads/encounters edition | Current signed learner, current valid relationship, current author disclosure and version applicability, specific Unit; encounter evidence separately recorded only when actual source operation is proven. | Reading is not automatic attendance, mastery, completion, accepted Work, submission or shared discussion. |
| Student writes personally | Exact learner-owned source and privately saved revision tied to actual lesson/prompt edition and an independent privacy rule. | Teacher/other student cannot see it. Atlas Notebook is a projection, not source ownership or automatic disclosure. |
| Learner deliberately shares/submits | Explicit student operation declaring *which* existing work, which recipient/audience, exact version, and purpose; source receipt preserved; no blanket transition of all Notebook contents. | Source-approved selected disclosure only. Separate private teacher feedback and shared Course discussion scopes. |
| Teacher reviews, learner revises | Exact teacher reviewer standing for that learner/work, original preserved, linked feedback and later learner attempt; true consequences separately issued if any. | Cannot overwrite student original, equate feedback with mastery, or expose private group work. |
| Author reissues and chooses per-learner edition | New author sealed edition with explicit per-learner continue-old or move-new decision; previous source encounter unchanged and reconstructible. | No silent replacement or automatic revocation of history. Every Class *released to that learner* remains freely navigable. |
| Withdrawal/revocation | Learner may leave under own authority; author may withdraw future material invitation/grant under governing law, without impersonating learner acceptance; current checks fail closed. | Original invitation, responses, revisions and receipts remain historically intact; future read vs retained-history rights must be separately settled. |

**Delivery modes** (asynchronous, external video, in-person) are ways of participating in real Sessions, **not** separate enrollment or Course originals. Institutional standing, if later established, is distinct from personal study and cannot retroactively imply an institutional student enrollment.

## 3. Explicit source boundaries for development

1. **Course identity:** reuse exactly the same SK 101 original and all historical revisions at protected canonical cutover; do not mint a replacement just to satisfy old Ledger-first creation.
2. **Author rights:** course creator ≠ generic current Login ≠ automatically institution governor. Author may offer study on own privately authored Course only after the actual source issuer exists and proves this jurisdiction.
3. **Student consent:** recipient's identity and meaningful acceptance are separately issued from author's invitation; no third party can accept for Marlene or Melody. No Person cloning/credential forgery.
4. **Disclosure:** accepted study ≠ sealed Unit read; an author must decide exact Unit edition/learner release. Revocation of either relation or author disclosure blocks new content reads.
5. **Edition:** a saved draft is not issued material. Current legacy Atlas academic Version is not automatically a snapshot of the manuscript/Activity text.
6. **Privacy:** your notes, another student's notes, an individual student submission, teacher feedback and course-wide discussions have distinguishable source owners/audiences; no transparent replication of private responses into a group thread.
7. **Work law:** neither authored Homework Unit, invitation, acceptance, reading, posted question nor teacher feedback automatically creates Work or completion in Atlas.
8. **Identity/data:** no real Person IDs, emails, credentials, research bodies, lesson manuscript text or private student notes may be embedded in public migration/tests.
9. **Storage/release:** exact new source operation (if necessary) is a shared source migration reviewed in **noel-core-db** and tested on a disposable protected clone; Titus/Atlas only consume signed, narrow DTOs after independent source release. **No new Titus table or service-role authorization**.
10. **Old sources:** no deletion/migration or legacy Course/Formation descendant retirement before separately authorized actual full-schema+data backup **and restore**, exact original-byte preservation and genuinely signed role tests.
