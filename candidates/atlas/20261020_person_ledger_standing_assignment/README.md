# Build 5 — Person–Ledger authority transition, Phase A

The one canonical relationship between a real Person and a native Ledger is `ledger.seats`. A position title or organizational membership may describe context; they do not confer receipt of Work. An employee delivery seat must confer neither authority nor assignment.

- Owner proposes a standing scope to an exact connected Person.
- The Person (not the organization) accepts once.
- Owner may directly allocate later in-scope Work without generating a question on every task.
- Allocation and result are carried by the existing governed `atlas.work_allocations` and `atlas.work_execution_results` records, not a separate employee task universe.
- An individual Work offer continues as a separate explicit-consent route.
- Schedule, Worker Day placement and notification receipt remain separate and are never inferred from assignment.

No role, employee classification, organization membership, paid seat or account is synthesized. No existing Work, credential, consent or personal history is deleted in Phase A.

This is an **additive transition**, not acceptance of a permanent compatibility layer: the user's required Phase B is physical removal of `atlas.organization_employee_seats` and associated legacy APIs once account custody is decoupled and fully tested.
