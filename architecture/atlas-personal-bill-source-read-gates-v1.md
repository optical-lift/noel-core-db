# Personal bill source read gates

A bill recognizer may receive structured email observations only after a verified Principal-owned Connected Source has the explicitly granted `personal.bill_notice.read` scope and `personalBillRecognition` capability. Ordinary communication capture, organization-owned sources, revoked connections, and missing coverage do not qualify. This is a proposed source-only membrane; it does not establish Money, bills, payments, or Clock work.

No production source is newly connected or read under this candidate.
