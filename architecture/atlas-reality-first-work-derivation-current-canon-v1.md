# Atlas Reality-First Work Derivation — Current Canon v1

**Status:** Governing architecture; storage-neutral in this tranche  
**Established:** September 29, 2026  
**Canonical repository:** `optical-lift/noel-core-db`  
**Governs:** the relationship among underlying reality, condition/state, requirements, Company Work, responsibility, execution authority, execution participation, Actuals, and continuation  
**Does not rewrite:** existing `atlas.work_items`, `atlas.work_allocations`, historical Work, historical allocations, domain-owned state, or present execution-warrant law

## 1. Why this correction exists

Atlas already has strong separation laws around Events, Claims, governed consequences, Requirements, responsibility, execution readiness, Clock placement, Actuals, and continuation. It also has a Company Work kernel in which Work identity exists independently from assignment.

Those laws remain useful, but one higher-order distinction was still implicit rather than constitutional:

> **The underlying reality does not begin as Work. Work is an operational carrier derived when a governed reality requires execution.**

Without this distinction, Atlas can accidentally make a Work row, Task, assignment, Today placement, or execution attempt become the identity of the thing that actually persists in reality.

That is false for both finite transformations and continuing charges.

This contract therefore places the Company Work kernel beneath a more primitive reality law.

## 2. Evidence basis and epistemic boundary

This correction was derived from Noel's verified Hebrew source witness and checked against the raw Mark/glyph research as a structural control.

The relevant Hebrew patterns include:

- Genesis 2:1–3 — `כלה` marks an end/completion condition and `שבת` marks cessation after the work;
- Genesis 39:4–6 — a household/domain persists while custody/governance is placed in Joseph's hand;
- Song 1:6 — being set as a keeper is distinct from whether the keeping operation was actually performed;
- Song 3:1–3 — the same unresolved referent/relation persists through repeated search attempts;
- Song 5:2, 4, 6–7 — performing a response operation does not guarantee that the governing outcome was achieved;
- Song 6:11 — inspection/seeing whether a state has changed is an observation operation, not creation of the underlying state;
- Song 8:11–12 — vineyard identity, possession/custody, keepers, fruit, and economic consequence remain distinguishable;
- Numbers 3:6–10 — placement, service, `משמרת`, and `עבודה` are layered rather than interchangeable;
- Numbers 8:24–26 — one execution/service mode can cease while participation in keeping a continuing `משמרת` remains;
- Genesis 39:4–6 — appointment/custody and the real household being governed remain distinct.

This architecture does **not** claim that one English Atlas term is the lexical meaning of any single Hebrew token. The evidence is used for functional distinctions and ordering.

### 2.2 Raw Mark/glyph boundary

## 3. Governing law

> **A real referent may stand in governed relations and have an actual configuration. A governing relation may require, permit, prohibit, preserve, or target a configuration or transition. When that governed reality calls for concrete execution, Atlas may derive an operational Work carrier. The Work carrier does not replace the referent, governing relation, requirement, actual state, or outcome.**

The universal ordering is therefore:

```text
REAL REFERENT / DOMAIN
    ↓
ACTUAL CONFIGURATION / STATE EVIDENCE
    +
GOVERNING RELATION / CHARGE / BOUNDARY
    +
REQUIRED, PERMITTED, PROHIBITED, OR PRESERVED CONDITION
    ↓
DOMAIN-OWNED RESOLUTION
    ↓
EFFECTIVE REQUIREMENT / CONSEQUENCE / ACTIONABLE CONDITION
    ↓ when concrete execution needs an operational carrier
COMPANY WORK / OTHER DOMAIN WORK CARRIER
    ↓
EXACT RESPONSIBILITY / CUSTODY
    ↓
LAWFUL RECIPIENT
    ↓
PRESENT EXECUTION AUTHORITY
    ↓
EXECUTION PARTICIPATION / OPERATION EVENT(S)
    ↓
ACTUAL / OBSERVATION / RESULT EVIDENCE
    ↓
DOMAIN-OWNED RESOLUTION
    ├─ requirement satisfied / finite closure
    ├─ state restored but continuing charge remains
    ├─ still unresolved
    └─ downstream continuation/reconsideration
```

Not every reality passes through every layer.

## 4. The persistent identity is not the attempt

A repeated attempt does not become the identity of the unresolved reality.

Forbidden collapse:

```text
attempt 1
→ failed
→ attempt 2
→ failed
→ attempt 3

therefore three unrelated realities
```

The persistent subject may instead be one referent/relation/condition whose resolution history contains several operations.

Therefore:

```text
Task identity
≠ underlying reality identity

Work identity
≠ underlying referent identity

execution attempt
≠ requirement identity

attention state
≠ requirement identity

Today placement
≠ requirement identity
```

Tasks and Work may be excellent operational carriers. They are not allowed to erase the thing whose state made execution necessary.

## 5. Observation is not state creation

Atlas must preserve the distinction between:

```text
reality has state S
```

and:

```text
observer inspected reality and obtained evidence about S
```

An inspection, review, check-in, measurement, message, or sensor event may supply evidence.

It does not make the proposition true merely because the observation occurred.

This extends the existing Reality Transition Protocol law:

```text
occurrence happened
≠ proposition established
```

to operational attention:

```text
inspection scheduled
≠ state unknown

inspection performed
≠ desired state established

Task completed
≠ inspected subject satisfactory
```

## 6. Governed condition precedes operational Work

The universal layer should not ask first:

> What Work exists?

It should be capable of asking:

> What reality exists, what is currently established about its condition, what governing relation applies, and what does that relation require next?

A domain resolver may derive an actionable condition from several shapes, including:

### 6.1 State discrepancy

```text
actual state S0
+ governing requirement expects S1
→ change/restoration requirement
```

### 6.2 Required transition

```text
current state S0
+ governing relation requires transition T
→ operation requirement
```

### 6.3 Preservation / keeping

```text
acceptable state S
+ continuing charge requires S remain within bounds
→ continuing governed condition
```

This condition may require no current corrective Work.

### 6.4 Evidence freshness / inspection

```text
continuing charge
+ governing rule requires current evidence
+ evidence is absent/stale under that rule
→ truth-acquisition / inspection requirement
```

The requirement arises from the governing rule, not from a generic assumption that everything must be checked periodically.

### 6.5 Prohibition / boundary protection

```text
governing boundary
+ proposed/actual transition would violate it
→ prevention, rejection, or repair consequence
```

The owning domain remains authoritative for each derivation.

## 7. Two temporal shapes must not share one completion law

### 7.1 Transformational reality

Some requirements aim at a finite target condition:

```text
S0
→ authorized operation(s)
→ S1
→ S1 established by sufficient evidence
→ requirement satisfied
→ execution may cease
```

A Work carrier may become terminal when its own operational lifecycle ends, but domain closure still requires the governing resolution law. Work terminality alone is not the proof.

### 7.2 Keeping / governing reality

Other relations persist while a domain must remain within an acceptable state envelope:

```text
active charge
→ observe/preserve/repair as warranted
→ acceptable state
→ charge still active
→ later observe/preserve/repair as warranted
```

Here, one Work item can be completed while the charge remains fully active.

Therefore:

```text
corrective Work completed
≠ continuing charge completed

inspection completed
≠ referent permanently satisfactory

current acceptable state
≠ no future operation can become necessary
```

A continuing charge ends only under its own governing termination/transfer law.

## 8. Reconciliation with the Company Work kernel

`architecture/atlas-company-work-kernel-v1.md` says:

> Work exists at the organization level before assignment.

That remains valid **inside the operational Company Work layer**:

```text
Company Work identity
→ may exist unassigned
→ later exact responsibility/custody may be established
```

This new higher law clarifies what precedes Company Work itself:

```text
underlying governed reality / requirement
→ may warrant creation or recognition of Company Work
→ Work then exists independently from assignment
```

Accordingly:

- `atlas.work_items` remains the canonical identity of Company Work once that operational Work exists;
- it is not automatically the canonical identity of the underlying domain referent, governing relation, condition, or requirement;
- `atlas.work_allocations` remains exact Company Work responsibility/custody;
- unassigned Work remains valid;
- Work history must not be rewritten merely because the underlying condition later changes;
- a domain may resolve a requirement without creating Company Work where no operational carrier is warranted.

No live rows are rewritten by this clarification.

## 9. Exact Work responsibility remains upstream of present execution authority

Existing canon remains:

```text
Company Work
→ exact Work responsibility / custody (`atlas.work_allocations`)
→ lawful recipient
→ present execution authority
```

The earlier experimental Atlas branch that used the name `WorkAllocationWarrant` **after** present execution authority is not canonical architecture. It would create a second meaning of allocation and reverse the established dependency.

That prototype may be retained as historical implementation evidence, but its vocabulary must not govern future work.

## 10. Post-authority participation is execution reality, not another allocation

After present execution authority, a separate question may become relevant:

> Is this exact Person actually participating in execution of this exact operational Work/operation under that authority, and in what bounded role/state?

That relation is narrower than exact responsibility and narrower than authority.

It does not establish:

- ownership of the underlying referent;
- durable institutional responsibility;
- exact Work responsibility/custody;
- authority beyond the warrant already established;
- a Task;
- Today/Clock placement;
- that an operation was performed;
- that the intended result occurred;
- that the underlying condition was resolved.

If Atlas needs a reusable read/warrant for this seam, the preferred architectural name is **Execution Participation**, not Work Allocation.

Execution Participation should be treated initially as a temporally bounded execution relation/history projection. This contract does not yet establish a universal persistence table for it.

## 11. Execution Participation must preserve ordered history

If an Execution Participation history membrane is implemented, it must preserve:

- exact Person identity;
- exact Institution/context where institution is relevant;
- exact responsibility basis;
- exact Work identity where Work is the operational carrier;
- exact target/referent or operation target;
- exact operation key;
- bounded participation role where role is meaningful;
- participation state and validity interval;
- authority basis;
- evidence/provenance;
- `effective_at`;
- `created_at` / recorded-at time;
- explicit `as_of` resolution.

Historical resolution must not allow a later-recorded backdated event to rewrite what Atlas could have established at an earlier `as_of`.

At minimum, an event is eligible for an as-of view only when both are true:

```text
effective_at <= as_of
created_at   <= as_of
```

Ambiguous independent active chains fail closed.

## 12. Participation is not performance

A Person may enter an execution relation and still perform no effective operation.

A Person may perform an operation and still fail to produce the required outcome.

Therefore:

```text
execution participation established
≠ operation performed

operation performed
≠ result accepted

result reported
≠ actual state established

Work terminal
≠ underlying requirement satisfied
```

Actual/result evidence returns to the owning domain resolver.

## 13. Completion and continuation compose

This contract strengthens, rather than replaces, the Reality Transition and Continuation protocols.

For a finite requirement:

```text
Actual / evidence
→ owning-domain resolver
→ requirement satisfied
→ operational Work may settle
→ dependent continuation may be discovered
```

For a continuing charge:

```text
Actual / evidence
→ state restored/confirmed
→ current corrective requirement may settle
→ governing relation remains active
→ later state/evidence change may derive new requirement
```

A downstream reconsideration is still not itself the downstream transition.

## 14. Attention, planning, Today, and Clock are projections

The following may change presentation or temporal placement without changing the underlying governed reality:

- priority;
- attention;
- inbox/review status;
- planning lane;
- Today selection;
- Day admission;
- Clock placement;
- reminder state;
- notification state.

These layers may answer when/where an already-grounded requirement should be surfaced or acted upon.

They do not create the requirement merely by selecting it.

This is the architectural basis for Atlas eventually answering:

> What must keep moving today so that the governed realities across this Person/Institution do not drift into preventable failure?

The answer must be derived from governed reality, not from whichever list currently contains the most Tasks.

## 15. Implementation consequence

The next implementation sequence is:

1. preserve existing domain referents, observations, conditions, consequences, and requirements as domain-owned truth;
2. make Work derivation/adoption explicitly traceable to an effective requirement or other lawful operational basis where practical;
3. keep `atlas.work_items` as operational Work identity and `atlas.work_allocations` as exact Work responsibility/custody;
4. keep lawful-recipient and present execution-authority membranes downstream of responsibility;
5. replace the experimental post-authority `WorkAllocationWarrant` vocabulary with an **Execution Participation** membrane;
6. make that membrane read-only/fail-closed until a universal persistence law is actually warranted;
7. keep performance/result/Actual evidence separate from participation;
8. let owning-domain resolution decide satisfaction, restoration, continuing charge, or further continuation;
9. only after those semantics hold should Today/Clock arbitrate attention and placement.

## 16. Hard invariants

1. Underlying reality is not created by a Task, Work row, Today placement, Clock placement, or reminder.
2. Work is an operational carrier, not a universal substitute for the referent or condition that warranted it.
3. Company Work exists independently from assignment once created.
4. `atlas.work_allocations` means exact Company Work responsibility/custody and is not redefined downstream of execution authority.
5. Responsibility, lawful recipient, execution authority, execution participation, operation, Actual, and accepted outcome remain distinct.
6. Observation does not create the state it reports.
7. Work terminality does not establish the underlying domain outcome.
8. Finite transformation and continuing keeping do not share one universal completion rule.
9. A continuing charge can remain active after every currently derived Work item is complete.
10. Unknown state dimensions may remain unknown; Atlas must preserve structure rather than name by convenience.
11. Later-recorded backdated history must not rewrite an earlier as-of view.
12. Ambiguity fails closed.
13. Domain authority remains with the reality being changed.
14. Existing historical Work/allocation truth is preserved.
15. No schema object is created merely because this architecture can name a conceptual distinction.
